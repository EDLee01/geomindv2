# Gap Analysis Skill

## Purpose
Identify research gaps from literature review to suggest innovative research directions.

## Process

1. **Analyze Literature**
   - Review the selected papers from literature search
   - Identify common themes and methods
   - Note limitations mentioned by authors

2. **Identify Patterns**
   - What methods are commonly used?
   - What data sources are typical?
   - What geographical areas are studied?
   - What time scales are considered?

3. **Find Gaps**
   Look for:
   - Understudied regions or time periods
   - Missing methodological approaches
   - Unexplored variable combinations
   - Scale limitations (spatial or temporal)
   - Lack of mechanism understanding

4. **Evaluate Feasibility**
   For each gap, assess:
   - Data availability (⭐ rating)
   - Methodological foundation
   - Innovation potential
   - Publication potential

## Output Format

```markdown
# 研究空白分析

## 候选 Gap 1: [Gap Title]
**依据**:
- [Paper X] 指出 "..."
- [Paper Y] 提到 "..."
**可行性**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ [Brief assessment]
**创新性**: [High/Medium/Low]

## 候选 Gap 2: [Gap Title]
...

---

💡 **头脑风暴**:
- [Suggestion 1]
- [Suggestion 2]
- 你对哪个方向更感兴趣？
```

## Guidance

- Present 3-5 candidate gaps
- Always cite specific papers for each gap
- Include direct quotes from abstracts when possible
- Encourage user discussion and preference
- Consider combining gaps for stronger innovation

## Questions to Ask User

- "你更关注方法创新还是应用创新?"
- "数据获取方面有什么限制?"
- "希望论文发表在什么级别的期刊?"
